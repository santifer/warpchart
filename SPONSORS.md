# Sponsors

Warpchart is free and open source. Sponsorship covers hosting and keeps every chart, badge and scan free for everyone.

## The one rule

**The data is never for sale.** Sponsors buy clearly labeled placements, never position, size, velocity or ranking. Nothing a sponsor pays for can alter what the telemetry shows. If the map could be bought, it would be worthless to everyone, sponsors included.

## Placements

| Tier | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| Patron | Name listed below and on [warpchart.dev/sponsors](https://warpchart.dev/sponsors) |
| Featured | Logo on the sponsors page plus a labeled FEATURED SCAN chip on the explore landing |
| Landmark | Top 1000 repos only: the repo's node on the route to the core carries its name and a sponsor glyph, at its real position. Maximum 4 slots |

Every sponsored placement is visibly labeled (SPONSOR). The house demo mission is labeled HOUSE and is not a paid placement.
